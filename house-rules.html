The Nine House Rules

Easy to Follow
badge
Registered Guests Only

Only guests whose names are on the booking may stay or enter the house. If you are expecting a visitor, tell us in advance so we can approve it.

smoke_free
No Smoking

This is a smoke-free home. Please do not smoke inside the apartment, on the balcony, or in any shared outdoor area.

celebration
No Parties or Events

Please do not host parties, group gatherings, or events here. The house is for small, quiet stays so the neighbourhood stays peaceful.

bedtime
Quiet Hours

Please keep noise down between 10:00 PM and 8:00 AM. This includes music, television, and conversations.

chair
Care of the Furnishings

Please treat the furniture, fabrics, and fittings as if they were your own. Keep food and drinks away from the beds and upholstery.

lightbulb
Switch Off Lights & Appliances

Turn off all lights, fans, air conditioning, and appliances when you leave the house or go to bed. It keeps our electricity costs down.

lock
Lock Doors and Gates

Lock the main door, the bedroom door, and all gates every time you leave — even for a short time out.

build
Report Damage or Breakages

If something breaks, stains, or stops working, please tell us as soon as possible so we can fix it straight away.

pets
Pets

Pets are not allowed on the property. If you need to bring one, please ask us first and get our approval before you arrive.

Thank You
